The problem

Most content fails because nobody decided what it was for.

The pattern is almost always the same. Someone decides the business should "do more content". A few posts go out. Nobody can tell whether they worked, so the next batch gets made to a different standard, at a different time, by whoever is least busy. Six months later there's a lot of material and no compounding effect.

The missing piece is never effort. It's the decision that should have come first — what this content is for, who it's aimed at, and what it's supposed to change. Get that right and the making becomes straightforward. Get it wrong and no amount of output rescues it.

We start with the decision, not the camera.

Working locally

Why being nearby still matters for content.

You can't shoot a business you've never stood inside.

Strategy can be done remotely. Production can't, not properly. The difference between content that looks like your business and content that looks like anyone's is usually a day spent on site — watching how the work actually happens, where the light is good, which member of staff is a natural on camera.

We work across Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch, the New Forest and the wider Dorset and Hampshire area, which means shoot days don't carry travel costs and a half-day visit is never a logistical event. For clients further afield we travel, but the local roster is deliberately the core of what we do.

What is content strategy, in plain English?

Deciding what your business should be saying, to whom, and how often — before anything gets made. In practice: an audit of what you have, a shortlist of stories only you can tell, a set of repeatable formats, and a calendar someone can realistically keep to.

How long before it produces results?

The strategy itself takes two to four weeks. Results from publishing against it are cumulative — most businesses see engagement shift within a couple of months and search visibility within three to six, provided publishing stays consistent. Anyone promising faster is guessing.

Do you write the content as well as plan it?

Yes, and we shoot it. A strategy nobody executes is just a document. See video production for the photo and film side.

Can you work with our existing marketing team?

Often that's the best arrangement. We build the strategy, templates and standards, then either run it or hand it over and train your team.

What does it cost?

Quoted per project after a short conversation — a one-off strategy and a twelve-month programme aren't comparable. You'll have a fixed number in writing before anything starts.
