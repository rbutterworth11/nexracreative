## Worth knowing

**These are placeholders with a job.** Stock is fine for launch, but a studio that
sells content production is judged on its own imagery. Replace these with your own
work as soon as you have shots you can show — it's the single biggest credibility
upgrade available to this site.

**`case-collectibles.jpg` shows branded football shirts** (club sponsors and player
names are legible). It's licensed stock used illustratively, not a client asset,
but if you'd rather nothing recognisable appears next to an anonymised case study,
a neutral alternative is
https://www.pexels.com/photo/vintage-varsity-jackets-in-a-locker-room-36328521/ —
same subject, no logos.
