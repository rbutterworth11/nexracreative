Do you work on retainer or per project?

Both. Websites and production are usually fixed-scope projects. Content and social work best as a monthly retainer, because consistency is the thing that actually produces results. Retainers run month to month with thirty days notice — there's no long tie-in.

How much does it cost?

Everything is quoted per project after a short conversation. You'll get a fixed number in writing before anything starts, and we'd rather tell you the budget doesn't fit than pad the scope to make it.

Do you only work with businesses in Dorset and Hampshire?

That's where most of our clients are and where shoot days are easiest, but we work across the UK. Strategy, web and SEO run perfectly well remotely; production days are travelled to.

Do you only work with certain industries?

No, but we work best with businesses that have something genuinely interesting to show — a craft, a product, an archive, real expertise. If the honest answer is that there's nothing to say yet, working that out is the first job.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Often that's the best arrangement. We build the strategy, templates and standards, then either run it or hand it over and train your team to keep it going.

How does a project actually start?

Enquiry, a thirty-minute call, then a short written proposal with a fixed price. Nothing is chargeable until you've signed that off. The full process.
